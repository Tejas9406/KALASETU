## 1. PROBLEM STATEMENT DEEP DIVE

### 1.1 Official Problem Statement

> **TUR05-Local Artisan and Experience Discovery Platform — Develop a platform connecting tourists with verified local artisans, cultural activities and community experiences.**

### 1.2 The Core Problem

India has 7+ million artisans, with 90%+ working in the informal sector. These artisans:
- Have no digital visibility → invisible to tourists
- Are exploited by middlemen taking 40-60% of profits
- Are aging (avg 55+ years) with no youth taking over → dying heritage
- Face language, literacy, and technology barriers
- 70%+ are women with mobility and safety constraints

**Tourists (Domestic & International):**
- Buy mass-produced "fake" souvenirs
- Can't find authentic workshops/experiences
- No trust/verification mechanism
- Language barriers with local artisans
- No way to book experiences in advance
- Safety concerns (especially women solo travellers)

**Government:**
- No unified platform for craft tourism
- Schemes like "One District One Product" (ODOP) lack digital reach
- No data on artisan-tourism economic impact
- GI-tagged products getting counterfeited
- SDG 8 (Decent Work) & SDG 12 (Sustainable Consumption)

### 1.3 Root-Cause Analysis

```
SYMPTOM: Tourists can't discover authentic local experiences
    ↓
WHY? Artisans have no digital visibility
    ↓
WHY? Low digital literacy, fragmented listings, no unified platform
    ↓
WHY? Existing platforms designed for commercial sellers, not artisans
    ↓
WHY? Local cultural experiences are heterogeneous & difficult to standardize
    ↓
WHY? No trusted, localized discovery + verification + booking ecosystem
    ↓
ROOT CAUSE: Fragmented government ecosystems + missing discovery layer
```

## 2. EXISTING SOLUTIONS & GAP ANALYSIS

### 2.1 What Already Exists in India

| Platform | What It Does | Gap |
|----------|--------------|-----|
| **LastClues** | Digital onboarding of rural tourism stakeholders (artisans, homestays, guides) across 15 states | No unified discovery/booking; focuses on digital identity |
| **OneTAC (Karnataka)** | Unified digital grid for tourism, arts, culture with verifiable credentials | Still in pilot; limited to Karnataka |
| **Craftgroom (MP Tourism)** | E-commerce platform for artisans | Product-focused; no tourism experiences/booking |
| **ODOP (One District One Product)** | Showcases district-specific products | No discovery/booking; just product showcase |
| **Urbanaut** | Local experiences app (feni tastings, pottery workshops, etc.) | Limited to select cities; no artisan verification |

### 2.2 What Already Exists Globally

| Platform | What It Does | Gap |
|----------|--------------|-----|
| **Airbnb Experiences** | Local experiences hosted by locals | No artisan verification; not India-specific |
| **Viator** | 400,000+ travel experiences | Mass-market tours; no artisan focus |
| **R FINDS (Renaissance Hotels)** | Commission-free marketplace for local artisans | Hotel-only; no experiences/booking |
| **Irish Hands** | Digital arts & crafts kiosk for discovery, booking, purchasing | Limited to Inishowen, Ireland |
| **Florence Artisan Platform** | Maps verified artisan workshops for tourists | Still conceptual; no booking |

### 2.3 The Gap We Fill

**Identified Gap:**
> "Existing solutions are fragmented. No unified platform combines **discovery + verification + booking + safety** for artisan experiences in India."

**Why Existing Solutions Don't Solve This:**
- **LastClues/OneTAC** → Digital identity but no discovery/booking
- **Airbnb/Viator** → Experiences but no artisan verification
- **Craftgroom/Etsy** → Products but no experiences
- **ODOP** → Showcase but no booking

**Our Opportunity:**
> Build the "Missing Discovery Layer" that connects tourists with verified artisans through:
> - Discovery & Booking
> - Evidence-based verification
> - Voice-first inclusive design
> - Government intelligence dashboard

## 10. ACCESSIBILITY & INCLUSION

### 10.1 Voice-First Design

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                          VOICE-FIRST DESIGN                                     │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│                                                                                 │
│  FOR TOURISTS:                                                                  │
│  ├── Voice search: "Find pottery workshops near Jaipur"                       │
│  ├── Voice translation: Real-time translation between                         │
│  │   tourist and artisan (Hindi ↔ English ↔ regional)                         │
│  └── Voice booking: "Book a block-printing workshop for tomorrow"            │
│                                                                                 │
│  FOR ARTISANS (ESPECIALLY ELDERLY & WOMEN):                                    │
│  ├── Voice-based listing creation (no typing needed)                          │
│  ├── Voice notifications: "You have a new booking request"                    │
│  └── Voice-first onboarding: "Tell us about your craft"                       │
│                                                                                 │
│  WHY THIS MATTERS:                                                             │
│  ├── 70%+ artisans are semi-literate or have low digital literacy             │
│  ├── Elderly artisans (60+) can't use complex interfaces                      │
│  └── Voice-first = truly inclusive platform                                   │
│                                                                                 │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```
